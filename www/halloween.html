Mr Owl's Halloween Cemetery

Cmentarz na Halloween · learn Polish by night

How to Play / Jak grać
  • Walk Mr Owl with the thumb-stick, by dragging on the map or with the arrow keys; tap a lit path to walk there
  • Monsters attack when they reach you: answer the Polish question to beat them
  • A wrong answer sends you back to the cemetery gate
  • Each small tomb's guardian holds a part of the skeleton key: find all four
  • Open the great tomb and beat the Grim Reaper with three right answers in a row
